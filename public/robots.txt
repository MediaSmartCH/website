# AI assistants fetching a page because someone asked about it.
# These are the retrieval agents, not the training crawlers: they fetch on a
# user's request, or index for an assistant's own search, so the assistant can
# cite the page in an answer. Allowing them is what makes the site quotable in
# ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude; the crawlers that collect pages to train a
# model stay blocked below.
#
# The names matter and they change. Claude-Web was Anthropic's old agent and no
# longer fetches anything; the two that do are Claude-User (a fetch on someone's
# request) and Claude-SearchBot (the index behind Claude's own search). Allowing

# request, OAI-SearchBot builds the index ChatGPT search reads.
