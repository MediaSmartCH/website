# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We expect contributors to communicate clearly, act professionally, and respect the time and safety of other participants.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Give technical feedback that is specific, factual, and respectful.
- Assume good intent while still challenging weak reasoning.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project for users and maintainers.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Personal attacks, harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior in issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that do not meet this standard.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact `security@mediasmart.ch`.
